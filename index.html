Serina Therapeutics · NYSE American: SER · Huntsville, Alabama

Steadier release for drugs we already know

Serina is a clinical-stage biotechnology company. Its POZ technology links existing drugs to a synthetic polymer, poly(2-oxazoline), engineered to give more control over how much drug is attached and how fast it is released after a long-acting subcutaneous injection. The lead candidate, SER-252, is apomorphine on that polymer, now in a Phase 1b registrational study in advanced Parkinson’s disease. 10-K, Mar 25, 2026

The platform

The drug sits on the polymer’s side chains, not only at its ends

POZ is a water-soluble, low-viscosity polymer made from 2-ethyl- or 2-methyl-2-oxazoline. Serina attaches copies of an existing drug to it, and the company believes this can let a single subcutaneous injection deliver the drug continuously. 10-K, Mar 25, 2026

  1. 01

    Attach

    Ten or more copies of the drug are bonded to the side chains of one polymer backbone. Higher drug loading per polymer means, the company says, that less polymer is needed to deliver the same amount of drug.

  2. 02

    Inject

    The conjugate is a long-acting subcutaneous injection. The company believes SER-252 may be given at home, as a single or twice-weekly injection, without a healthcare provider.

  3. 03

    Release

    Programmable linkers let the drug come off the polymer slowly. The company reports that in animal studies POZ programmed the release of small molecules. For SER-252 in people, the first look is the blinded Cohort 1 pharmacokinetic profile, which it calls consistent with prolonged apomorphine exposure. 8-K, Sep 9, 2026

What the company says sets POZ apart from PEG

  • 01

    More drug per chain

    PEG carries one or two copies of a drug, at its ends. POZ carries ten or more along the side chain.

  • 02

    No antibodies to the polymer, so far

    Repeat dosing of POZ in rabbits did not generate anti-POZ antibodies, and the Phase Ia study of SER-214 showed no toxicity concerns.

  • 03

    Cleared, not stored

    In preclinical IND-enabling studies in rodents and monkeys, POZ was cleared by the kidneys as an intact polymer, with no evidence of accumulation in any tissue. The company says PEG is known to accumulate in several tissues.

  • 04

    Simple to make and keep

    POZ is made in essentially one pot from low-cost monomers, and drug-POZ conjugates can be stored at room temperature or in a refrigerator. PEG starts from ethylene oxide, a flammable, toxic gas.

A limit on all of this. The company has not compared PEG-conjugated and POZ-conjugated molecules in humans. Its comparisons are in vitro and in animals. 10-K, Mar 25, 2026

The need

Oral doses wear off, and the continuous options use a catheter or a pump

Most people diagnosed with Parkinson’s disease are treated with oral levodopa and carbidopa. The 10-K says that in almost all cases of oral treatment patients may experience “wearing off” or a disabling side effect called dyskinesia, and that most oral drugs deliver dopamine stimulation in peaks and troughs. 10-K, Mar 25, 2026

Lead program

SER-252: cohorts of eight, then sixteen

SER-252 is apomorphine, a dopamine agonist, attached to POZ. The SER-252-1b study is randomized, double-blind and placebo-controlled, in adults with Parkinson’s disease and motor fluctuations. It tests safety, tolerability and pharmacokinetics first, with exploratory measures of motor function. 8-K, Sep 9, 2026

Cohort 1, in the company’s words

“blinded Cohort 1 observations showed a pharmacokinetic profile consistent with the sustained apomorphine exposure SER-252 is designed to provide and included sustained periods of improvement in motor function in individual patients on exploratory clinical measures.”

Serina, Sep 9, 2026 press release. 8-K, Sep 9, 2026

Context the release also gives: during Cohort 1, patients were withdrawn from background Parkinson’s medications under the protocol, so that investigators could characterize pharmacokinetics and motor function with limited contribution from other dopaminergic therapy. Cohort 1 was eight patients at the lowest dose, randomized 3:1 to SER-252 or placebo.

Partners and the platform beyond SER-252

One delivery partner for SER-252, one license for the platform

Serina’s business is largely a wholly owned pipeline of POZ-enabled drug candidates, and it intends to take other uses of POZ forward through out-licensing, co-development and other partnerships. 10-K, Mar 25, 2026

Delivery partner · May 2024

Enable Injections

In May 2024 Serina entered a partnership with Enable Injections, Inc. to develop and commercialize SER-252 together with enFuse, a wearable drug delivery system for at-home self-administration. The company describes enFuse as designed to overcome the shortcomings of IV infusion and of other subcutaneous methods. 10-K, Mar 25, 2026

Earlier candidate

SER-214

POZ-rotigotine for early Parkinson’s disease and restless legs syndrome. It completed a Phase Ia study in 19 subjects as a proof of principle for POZ in humans, is not being advanced internally, and may be out-licensed.

License · October 2023

Pfizer

A non-exclusive license to use POZ in lipid nanoparticle drug delivery formulations, for a specific POZ polymer structure in one field. It followed two years of work, from early 2021, with several large pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies under agreements the 10-K calls FSAs, on POZ-lipids as a replacement for PEG-lipids in mRNA vaccines.

Other uses · intent to license

RNA delivery and antibody-drug conjugates

The company intends to license POZ to others developing lipid nanoparticle approaches in infectious diseases, cancer immunology and gene therapy, and describes POZ as applicable to antibody-drug conjugates. None of these programs is the company’s own.

The company’s 8-K press releases, most recently on Mar 25, 2026, describe SER-270 as an investigational once-weekly VMAT2 inhibitor for tardive dyskinesia and say the company is also exploring Huntington’s disease chorea. The Nov 13, 2025 release said it was on track to complete formulation optimization and pre-IND activities in 2026, while the investor site still says it is anticipated to enter IND enabling studies in the fourth quarter of 2025. The 10-K does not name it, so it is not counted in the pipeline above. 8-K, Nov 13, 2025 8-K, Mar 25, 2026

Disclosures

What the filings say about risk

Seven matters a reader should have in front of them. Each is in the company’s own filings, and each carries its source.

01

Going concern

“Management believes that its cash and cash equivalents of $23.0 million as of June 30, 2026 are not expected to be sufficient to satisfy the Company’s anticipated operating and other funding requirements for the twelve months from the issuance of these unaudited condensed consolidated interim financial statements. As such, there is substantial doubt about the Company’s ability to continue as a going concern.”

The 10-K for 2025 reached the same conclusion when cash was $3.1M, and its risk factors say the auditor’s report contains a qualification to that effect. The company expects to rely largely on raising money from equity investors. 10-Q, Note 1 10-K, Mar 25, 2026

02

Funding and dilution

Shares outstanding rose from about 10.8 million at Dec 31, 2025 to 25,400,473 at Aug 10, 2026. The March 2026 private placement came with redeemable warrants at $5.00 a share and pre-funded warrants, and the company has since agreed an equity line of up to $25.0 million at a discount to market. The company says the exercise of outstanding warrants and other issuances could result in substantial dilution. 10-Q, Aug 13, 2026 8-K, Aug 27, 2026

03

NYSE American listing

On Jan 9, 2026 the exchange told the company it was below its stockholders’ equity requirements: about $1.6 million at Sep 30, 2025 against $2.0 million and $4.0 million thresholds. The exchange accepted the company’s compliance plan on Mar 24, 2026, which permits the stock to remain listed during a plan period ending Jul 9, 2027, subject to periodic review including quarterly monitoring. The company says there can be no assurance it will maintain compliance, and that delisting proceedings may follow if it does not. Equity was about $18.7 million at Jun 30, 2026. 8-K, Jan 15, 2026 10-Q

04

Two closely aligned large holders

On Aug 10, 2026 Juvenescence Limited beneficially owned about 17% of the common stock and Gregory H. Bailey, a director and Co-Chairman, about 41%, each counting shares issuable on exercising warrants. Dr. Bailey is Juvenescence’s executive chairman and a co-founder, and another director is its chief executive. The company says their interests are closely aligned and that, if they act together, they can control or substantially influence stockholder votes, and that conflicts of interest may arise. The 10-K gave Juvenescence about 33.0% on Mar 1, 2026, before the March 2026 placement. The lead investor in that placement was also a board member. 10-Q, Aug 13, 2026 10-K, Mar 25, 2026

05

The clinical hold, now lifted

The FDA placed a clinical hold on the SER-252 IND on Nov 3, 2025, and Serina announced on Jan 28, 2026 that the FDA had cleared it. The company says the FDA’s feedback concerned a commonly used excipient and did not relate to the active drug substance or its mechanism. It also warns that the FDA approval process is lengthy and unpredictable, and that delays are possible. The dated record

06

No product revenue yet

Serina’s revenue so far is grant revenue: $130 thousand in 2025 and none in the first half of 2026. The company says it is uncertain when, if ever, it will realize revenue from product sales, and that it expects to rely largely on raising capital from equity investors, with some funding possibly from licensing agreements. 10-Q, Aug 13, 2026

07

Early, small and blinded

Cohort 1 enrolled eight patients, randomized 3:1 to SER-252 or placebo, at the lowest dose of a Phase 1b study. The company says preliminary results from its nonclinical studies and clinical trials may change as more patient data becomes available and the data is audited and verified. 10-K, Mar 25, 2026 8-K, Sep 9, 2026
